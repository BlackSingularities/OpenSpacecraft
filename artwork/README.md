Design a compact polished sculptural space probe inspired by deep-space exploration: a large ivory and silvery high-gain dish with a tiny gold central feed, a modest warm-gold spacecraft body and a pair of small dark blue solar panel wings, together with a single clean cyan orbital arc sweeping around a small deep-blue planet. No words, letters, numbers, logos, frames, tiles, squares, disk, sky, stars background, outer ground, or cast shadow. Isolated emblem on true transparent background, centered with ample margins.
